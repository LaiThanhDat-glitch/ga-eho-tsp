# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want participation in the GA-EHO-TSP project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Contributors should communicate in good faith, consider differing perspectives, and focus technical discussions on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with respect and use clear, considerate language.
- Give constructive feedback and explain technical concerns with evidence where possible.
- Credit the work and ideas of others accurately.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal or confidential information without permission.
- Report code, data, and experiment results honestly, including limitations and unsuccessful outcomes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted disclosure of private information, and knowingly misrepresenting another person's contributions or experimental results are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code applies to project spaces and activities, including repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions in which a participant represents the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the project lead, Trương Thành Đạt, through an established private team contact. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. The project lead will review reports as confidentially as practical, hear from the people involved, and decide on a proportionate response. Responses may include a request to stop or correct behavior, removal of inappropriate content, or limits on participation.

If the report concerns the project lead, contact another team member or the course supervisor through an established private channel.
